By Addison Graham. Credited to Lilly Graham.

Coverage by county and credential

This search lists people with a public Illinois office on the selected school year. Teachers of the Visually Impaired start selected. Other credentials appear when those boxes are checked. It shows the ten closest offices. It does not show home addresses or personal phone numbers.

Distance is measured to the employer’s public office. For a cooperative or a special-education joint agreement, that office may not be the town where the teacher sees students. A Chicago Public Schools row uses a school building when one was on the 2023–2024 log. Otherwise it says “district office.”

Example: 62704, Springfield, or 100 W Randolph St, Chicago, IL 60601.
