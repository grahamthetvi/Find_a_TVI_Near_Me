Find a TVI Near Me

By Addison Graham. Credited to Lilly Graham.

This search lists Teachers of the Visually Impaired who have a public Illinois employer on the 2024–2025 log. It shows the ten closest offices. It does not show home addresses or personal phone numbers.

Distance is measured to the employer’s public office. For a cooperative or a special-education joint agreement, that office may not be the town where the teacher sees students. A Chicago Public Schools row uses a school building when one was on the earlier log. Otherwise it says “district office.”

Example: 62704, Springfield, or 100 W Randolph St, Chicago, IL 60601.
